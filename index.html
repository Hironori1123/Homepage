01 / ABOUT

About

I am a phonologist whose research spans theoretical and experimental phonology, phonetics, and psycholinguistics, with a primary focus on Japanese linguistics. I use corpus analysis, laboratory experiments, acoustic analysis, and computational modeling to investigate phonological knowledge and linguistic variation.

Before joining the University of Canterbury, I was a Visiting Assistant Professor at the University of Kansas and a Postdoctoral Fellow at the University of Toronto Scarborough. I received my PhD in Linguistics from UCLA in 2023.
